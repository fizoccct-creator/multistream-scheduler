OBS Studio

Lets a scheduled stream play inside one of your OBS scenes, so your overlays and logos are included. In OBS, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, turn on Enable WebSocket server, then click Show Connect Info to see the password.

Use this app as a panel in OBS: in OBS, choose Docks → Custom Browser Docks, name it "Scheduler" and enter http://localhost:3030.
